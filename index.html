About

A surgeon's practice built on precision, training and trust

I am a General Surgeon with an MS in General Surgery from Kaloji Narayana Rao University of Health Sciences, and an MBBS from Osmania Medical College. I currently serve as Associate Professor in the Department of General Surgery at Government Medical College, Khammam.

My surgical training took place in high-volume teaching hospitals — including a 250-bed general surgery department at Chalmeda Anand Rao Institute of Medical Sciences, Karimnagar, with a daily case load of 100–120 patients. I have managed surgical emergencies, polytrauma (ATLS protocols), oncological emergencies, laparoscopic procedures, and complex abdominal and soft-tissue cases.

Beyond the operating theatre, I teach postgraduate and undergraduate students, present at surgical conferences, and believe every patient deserves a clear explanation of their condition, their options, and their recovery plan.
